How to Get Followers on Pinterest Fast in 2026

By Dipanshi Yadav  ·  June 2026  ·  8 min read

Growing on Pinterest is one of the best things you can do as a beauty and fashion creator. Unlike Instagram, Pinterest shows your pins to new people for months and even years after you post them. That means every pin you create today can bring you followers and sales for a long time. Here are my best tips to grow your Pinterest followers fast! 🌸

Tip 01

Complete Your Profile Perfectly

Your Pinterest profile is the first thing people see. Make sure it looks professional and complete. Use a clear photo of yourself as your profile picture. Write a bio that tells people exactly what you share — for example: "Beauty & Fashion lover sharing the best Amazon India finds 🌸 New pins daily!"

Most importantly, add your website link in your profile. This helps people trust you and visit your site where they can shop your affiliate picks!

✨ Quick Tip: Use keywords like "skincare", "fashion", "Amazon finds India" in your bio so Pinterest shows your profile in search results!

Tip 02

Pin Every Day Consistently

Pinterest loves creators who pin regularly. The more you pin, the more Pinterest shows your content to new people. Try to pin 3 to 5 times every day for best results.

The best times to pin are 8am and 8pm when most people are scrolling Pinterest. You don't have to create all new content — you can also save other people's pins to your boards to stay active!

✨ Quick Tip: Use the Pinterest scheduler to plan your pins in advance so you never miss a day!

Tip 03

Create Beautiful Pin Images

Pinterest is a visual platform — beautiful images get more clicks and followers. Use bright, clean, high quality images for your pins. The best pin size is 1000 x 1500 pixels (tall rectangle).

You can create stunning pins for free using Canva (canva.com). Add text on your images like "Best Vitamin C Serum Under ₹300 🧴" to tell people exactly what the pin is about!

✨ Quick Tip: Use pink, rose and cream colors in your pins to match the DY Fashion aesthetic and make your profile look consistent!

Tip 04

Use the Right Keywords

Pinterest works like a search engine — people search for things they want and Pinterest shows them relevant pins. So using the right keywords is very important!

In your pin title and description always include keywords like:

For Skincare: skincare routine India, best serum for glowing skin, affordable skincare Amazon India, vitamin c serum review

For Fashion: affordable fashion India, Amazon fashion finds, outfit ideas India, summer outfits 2026

✨ Quick Tip: Type your topic in the Pinterest search bar and look at the suggested words that appear — those are the most searched keywords! Use them in your pins.

Tip 05

Create Multiple Boards with Good Names

Boards help organise your pins and help Pinterest understand what your account is about. Create separate boards for each topic you cover. Good board names for DY Fashion:

🧴 "Best Skincare Products Amazon India"
💄 "Affordable Makeup Finds 2026"
👗 "Fashion Outfits Under ₹1000"
✨ "Glowing Skin Routine"
🌸 "Beauty Tips for Indian Skin"

✨ Quick Tip: Write a description for each board using keywords. This helps Pinterest show your boards in search results and brings more followers!

Tip 06

Follow Similar Accounts

One of the fastest ways to get followers is to follow people who are interested in beauty and fashion. Follow accounts that pin similar content to yours. Many of them will follow you back!

Also comment on popular pins in your niche. When people see your thoughtful comments they will visit your profile and follow you. Try to leave 5-10 genuine comments every day!

✨ Quick Tip: Don't just say "nice pin!" — write helpful comments like "I tried this serum and it really works for dark spots!" This builds trust and gets more profile visits!

Tip 07

Create Idea Pins for More Reach

Idea Pins (also called Story Pins) are Pinterest's newest feature and Pinterest pushes them to more people right now. Create idea pins showing:

🌟 Before & After skincare results
👗 Outfit of the day styling tips
🧴 Step by step skincare routine
💄 Get ready with me videos

Idea pins get shown to people who don't follow you yet — which means new followers every day!

✨ Quick Tip: You don't need a professional camera! Your phone camera is enough. Just make sure you have good lighting — natural sunlight works best!

Tip 08

Share Pinterest on Other Platforms

Don't rely only on Pinterest to grow. Share your Pinterest profile link everywhere:

📱 WhatsApp status — share your best pins daily
📸 Instagram bio — add your Pinterest link
💬 Telegram channel — share your pins there
👥 Facebook groups — share in beauty & fashion groups

The more places you share, the faster you grow!

✨ Quick Tip: Every time you add a new product to your DY Fashion website, create a pin for it and share it on WhatsApp and Instagram. This brings traffic from multiple sources!

Quick Summary — 8 Tips to Grow on Pinterest

  • ✦ Complete your profile with photo, bio and website link
  • ✦ Pin 3-5 times every day at 8am and 8pm
  • ✦ Create beautiful 1000x1500px pin images on Canva
  • ✦ Use keywords in every pin title and description
  • ✦ Create multiple boards with keyword-rich names
  • ✦ Follow similar accounts and leave genuine comments
  • ✦ Create Idea Pins for maximum reach
  • ✦ Share your Pinterest on WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram
